Risks and challenges
Developing Pantheon of Oracles is an ambitious project that blends AI
technology, astrology, and interactive storytelling. While I have already
built a working GPT foundation, expanding this into a fully gamified app
comes with challenges, including:
AI & Game Development Complexity – Ensuring smooth and dynamic
Oracle interactions requires fine-tuning AI models and integrating them
into an intuitive user experience.
Multiplayer & Social Features – Building a seamless social experience
where users can engage with their oracles and friends requires
additional backend infrastructure and testing.
Scalability & Performance – AI-driven systems require cloud computing
resources, which means optimizing performance to ensure a smooth,
real-time experience for all users.
I have committed myself full-time to learning AI development and
working through these challenges. With the funding from this campaign,
I will ensure that Pantheon of Oracles reaches its full potential.
I am dedicated to full transparency and regular updates throughout
development. Backers will receive exclusive insights, early access, and
opportunities to provide feedback as the project evolves.
Your support makes this possible, and I am excited to bring this vision to
life with you!
