Story
👋 Happy Friday! Feliz Viernes! (Feh-lees Byehr-nehs)!
I, my wife and my daughter are foodies. We always have a lot of fun when
we have food together, whether we're in a restaurant, or the food court at a
shopping mall, just having dinner at home, or even trying to cook together!
It's our time to just chill, and have fun as a family! Our happiness revolves
around food. 😁
My daughter recently started learning coding and started to compare
typical things you would see in the kitchen, like a blender and relate them
to coding concepts, like a library! They both essentially help to solve
problems, so you don't have to solve them yourself or build a solution from
scratch! Just like a blender blends, so you don't have to manually blend
ingredients together; a library is code already put together that you can
just reuse to solve a coding problem!
We thought it was a great idea, and we slowly found a lot of similarities in
the kitchen and cooking to coding concepts. We compiled a list and
worked together to design a video to share some concepts, while making a
simple blueberry pancake smoothie! Here's our first video -

We are going to try to release a new video every Friday on our website
https://cookingpy.com (check it out! we had a lot of fun designing this
together), to share some Spanish or Python coding concepts while cooking
deliciously simple recipes. We would love your support to keep us going,
this just eggs Abby and us to whip more videos so we all can have some
fun learning a new language or coding, while cooking!
Risks and challenges
Today, the three of us; I, my wife and my daughter, design all the videos
and the website end to end. We want to make it as fun and as engaging as
possible for everyone of all ages, and that's our biggest challenge to
ensure we can consistently deliver a fun user experience with every new
content.
We always take feedback from our friends and family, and incorporate
them on a regular basis. We would love to hear from those who would love
our initiative and would support our project. We want everyone to have fun
learning something new, while also having fun cooking!
