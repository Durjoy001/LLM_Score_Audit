Hitch is aiming for a low cost travelling but with no compromise in safety

Whatʼs in for the guest that uses Hitch?
• Guests will get to travel at the cheapest cost
• Guests will get the benefit of choosing their hosts based on other usersʼ
reviews
• Guest will get to choose their preferred routes
Whatʼs in for the guest? Whatʼs in for the host?
• Hosts will get extra reward points
• Hosts get to choose who they want to invite
• Hosts get to listen to all of the cool stories of their guests
