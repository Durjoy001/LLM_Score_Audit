Three Problems: One Solution
Introducing a revolutionary product that will help the music industry as a
whole one song at a time. Welcome to the world of Mi3. This nifty little
app is one that will bridge the gap between customer, DJ and artist.
Instead of me boring you for the next few minutes with technical mumbo
jumbo. I'm going to give you three scenarios and how Mi3 bring the
benefits.
First lets meet Angel, an aspiring artist. What became a hobby for her
ended up becoming a passion. She began singing at age 8 and where a
lot of people usually start off…by singing in the shower. Her mom heard
her one day and set her up to sing at the family picnic the following month.
From then, her dream became a reality. She spoke to several friends and
family members and she was told the best way to get her songs out there
was to get them on iTunes. Sounds like a plan right? Well it didn't go so
well because she was never noticed. Not only that, she never got a hit.
She was competing against big names like Rihanna and Beyonce…who
would want to hear Angel from Toronto?
Next lets meet John…he is what they call a bedroom DJ. He has a
constant booking every night…in his bedroom. It has always been his
dream to play in a club in front of real people instead of his stuffed animals
and his poster of David Guetta. His good friend advice him to create a mix
tape and post it online and that will do the trick…that the bookings will
come pouring in and people will fall in love with his work. So he did just
that and one week later…he posted it on a mix tape website and waited for
the phone to ring. Two weeks pass and nothing. He decides to go out for
a drive to clear his head and ended up at a local flea market and decides to
walk around. Suddenly while walking down the row, he hears something
familiar…it sounds like his mix tape. He runs and follows the music and it
brings him to a booth selling cds and his mix tape is playing and his
artwork is displayed on the counter. There is a big crowd and they are all
grooving to his work. “Wow there is hope” he thinks to himself. When the
crowd clears, he approaches the vendor and asks…”wow they love my
work eh?” The vendor replies “Yup I made about 200.00 today alone…
great work.” Now John starts to wonder…he put about two nights into
that mix tape and never got anything for it. “So how much of that 200.00 is
mine “ he asked the vendor. “Umm none, it was free online and by the
way, Iʼm promoting you so donʼt worry.” John is skeptical but believes that

NOW THE RESULTS!
All three scenarios sound way too common…more common than you
think. Here is where Mi3 cmes in. Mi3 is an all in one music app that is
unique…unique in the way it benefits not only the consumer but also the
DJ and the Artist to get his/her talent out into the world. So how does this
help you may ask?
Well for Angel, the aspiring artist…it promotes her music with all its app
users as well as give the user the ability to purchase her work and in turn
she not only makes money but also gets the attention she deserves. Also
her music can be promoted via popular social media outlets like
FACEBOOK, TWITTER, ETC.
For John, our DJ, Using Mi3 not only will he be able to make money with
his mixes, it will prevent bootlegging and also if is work is liked…he can be
booked right on the app itself. Also with the standard CD..its 80
mins...John can go as much as 100 mins without issues.
