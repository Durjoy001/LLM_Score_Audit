THE PRODUCT
Haven – A Mother’s Village is a community-networking App to help
mitigate this problem and help build the village that is vital to mothers and
families. Currently in MVP development and preparing to launch in 2026,
Haven will welcome all community members to join. The goal of the
platform will be focused on building small-localized diverse villages to
support mothers and families and build a safe Haven. Mothers, fathers and
caregivers at any stage of parenthood will be able to benefit from Haven.
A prototype of Haven was released in late 2025 to a select group of users
for feedback. 94% of users use community Apps to connect with parents
and families daily, but still feel unsupported. With this data and research
from the prototype, Haven has generated a growing following and waitlist
of potential users.
There is significant market opportunity with over 9 million mothers in
Canada, and a 4.5 billion dollar parenting market. With this, we have
future prospects to expand into the underserved maternal market in the
United States.

HAVEN WELLNESS HUB - PAID SUBSCRIPTION
In my second pregnancy and postpartum, I went to every physical support
specialist you can imagine to help me deal with the debilitating daily pain I
was facing. I saw physical therapists, pelvic floor therapists, chiropractors,
osteopaths, acupuncturists - you name it. Without my private insurance,
this would have cost me thousands of dollars. I thought to myself, how can
other mothers and families access these services, at a more reasonable
cost?
The paid subscription section of Haven is the Wellness Hub, which is one
of the final few sections of the App that still needs to be developed. It is
open to all Haven community members that are mothers or would like to
support mothers and families. The vision behind the Wellness Hub is to
offer affordable access to resources and services, available to all types of
mothers and families. The goal is to assist and help others heal,
emotionally, physically and mentally. There will be 3 pillars to the Wellness
Hub, which include:
Nervous System Regulation
Body & Physical Support
Trauma Release
The Wellness Hub will take you through a questionnaire. It will then give
you a result and an area of focus, based on these three pillars. Subscribers
may need support in one or all three pillars.
To ensure Haven subscribers get the most benefits out of this section, new
topics will be released each month of the year to encompass the three
pillars highlighted above i.e. April = "sleep support." New videos, podcasts
from professionals and exercises will be posted monthly, highlighting the
experienced coaches and brand partnerships we work with and access to
some of their incredibly creative resources, content and services. The
videos and resources can be saved in the "Library" section of the Haven
App to access at any time.
Haven will be partnering and promoting many Canadian ambassadors
and small organizations to help foster maternal and familial wellness. This
high engagement platform is bringing together families, local volunteers
and businesses for real life support.
WHY SUPPORT HAVEN?
This project is a creative idea to support community. What better way to
support community, than to reach out to all of you to help support and
share our vision. It is a group effort! We can build a better world, one
small act of kindness at a time.
