About the Game
"Rise of the Penguins" is a 2D side-scrolling platformer for the Game Boy.
Simple controls yet challenging, it features tough enemies and draws
inspiration from classics like Ghosts 'n Goblins and Mega Man.

Explanation of campaign
Description
Main reason to do the Kickstarter in the first place would be overhauling
the backgrounds and making the game look more professional. The sprites
won't be overhauled though but I believe by changing the backgrounds it
will change the way the game looks and will be cost effective way at
making the game look at lot better. I am always open to the level design on
tweaks and things that need to be changed.
I am not sure how much potential this game has but I want to try and get it
towards that 5000-10,000 download range by a combination of hard work,
good levels and features like fun mechanics as well as having decent
artwork that can make the game more appealing to people who prefer nice
looking art work.

Main goals
300$ is main goal to overhaul all of the backgrounds of the 6 main levels in
the game. This is going to cover the costs to pay 1 artist to do all 15 of them
and if there are funds that remain than additional backgrounds.
all 6 levels backgrounds will be done to look more professional
potential to draw more attention and reach new audiences to folks who
are turned off by the way the game looks currently compared to other
gameboy homebrew games.
if funds allow maybe can also overhaul the backgrounds in my "dlc
sized expansion"
500$ is stretch goal to get the game on steam and it will be there for free. I
don't have any prior experience porting .gb games to steam so I will be
using the bubble wrap tool. If there's a system that you want me to port the
game to and the tools are free I don't mind porting that and just adding it to
the itch page.
2000$ is goal if wanted to overhaul the sound. I don't have anyone in mind
at the moment who could do the music but from research the cost is high.
Therefore the stretch goals is more of a dream.
main music for all 6 levels
boss music if funds allow separate boss music for certain bosses
Future Plans
The future plans of the game regardless of whether or not the Kickstarter
gets fulfilled are
2 extra levels that will be accessible in this hub area
1 secret boss for getting all the fish throughout the 6 main levels
boss rush mode that will include all of the bosses and mini bosses in the
game
something in the ghost n goblins series is reimagining the levels
because you normally have to play the game twice to get the true
ending. I don't have any plans at the moment to reimagine the existing
levels but it's something I am thinking about but will have to see after I
get this "dlc sized expansion" completed.
Contacted about physical release but no signs of interest
As for a physical release I have contacted various companies that do the
physical releases but haven't heard anything back from them. But if I do
and am able to make a deal with them I will restart the campaign and
include the physical release into the rewards.
