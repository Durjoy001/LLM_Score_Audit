🏋 WHY I BUILT ZIOU'S GYM
Jun 5 2026 - Jun 30 2026 (25
days)
When I started going to the gym, I was like
most beginners: motivated, but completely lost.
I had no idea which exercises to do. 
I had no idea how to perform them correctly.
I had zero tracking of my sets, weights,
and repetitions.
And inevitably... I quit. Discouraged.
---
🔥 THE BIRTH OF ZIOU'S GYM
I decided things had to change.
I built Ziou's Gym for MYSELF first.
An app that shows me the correct technique
through video demonstrations for every exercise.
An app that tracks every set, every rep,
every kilogram I lift.
An app that helps me train with purpose
and stay consistent.
It completely changed how I train.
---
