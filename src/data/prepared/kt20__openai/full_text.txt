A fully featured open source brand-new Document Translations Portal. It
will be used by anyone in the world, translators and people in need of
translations alike, with no geographic or cultural restrictions. 
Why it's so important:
Quality document translations services are expensive or very expensive
while the need for such services is gradually rising, both in communities
and among government bodies. What we have is a clear situation of an
inadequate demand/supply case. The lion share of expenses for
translation services can be attributed to mark-ups of translations agencies
– the middlemen – while translators themselves are usually compensated
at a considerably lower level.
This is why this unique portal that will automatically connect translators
and clients (be it individuals, communities, corporations or
governments) is a clear win-win solution that will tremendously benefit
both sides!
The idea is to let clients post their projects for free and let qualified
translators bid on them. In the end, client selects translator(s) to provide

Features and benefits: The Portal will

Is easy to use with intuitive design and rich list of features

Reliable proprietary Escrow system ensuring mutual fairness and

approve their work; on the other hand, the system ensures that clients
have appropriate funds to pay for projects and that translators get paid
upon completion of projects
Proprietary public forums

Extensive system to rate translators and clients based on quality of their
