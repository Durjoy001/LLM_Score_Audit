The Vision
CommonLight is an AI-powered app that brings together teachings from
the Bible, Quran, Vedas, Torah, Tripitaka, Guru Granth Sahib, Tao Te
Ching, and more — all in one place.
Ask any question — about love, suffering, purpose, or doubt — and
CommonLight will show how different faiths illuminate the same truth from
their unique perspectives.
Itʼs not about converting.
Itʼs about connecting.
📱
How It Works
Ask a Question — e.g., “What is the nature of God?” or “How should I
handle anger?”
AI Retrieves Real Teachings — directly from multiple scriptures, verified
by translation and context.
Listen and Reflect — each answer comes with AI-generated narration for
a calm, reflective experience.
Filter and Explore — use Smart Tags to dive into topics like forgiveness,
compassion, destiny, and faith.
Stay Notified — with real-time updates when your personalized answers
and audio are ready.

Risks and challenges
Infrastructure and Scalability
AI queries and audio generation require stable cloud infrastructure. Rapid
growth could increase hosting costs.
🛠 Mitigation: Weʼre using scalable cloud architecture with efficient
caching and batching, ensuring costs remain predictable.
AI Accuracy and Scripture Integrity
Because CommonLight uses AI to retrieve verses from multiple religious
texts, thereʼs a small risk of misclassification or partial context.
🛠 Mitigation: We use only verified public-domain translations to ensure
every verse is correctly sourced and presented neutrally.

What parts of your project will use AI generated content? Please be as
specific as possible.
CommonLight uses AI for scripture retrieval and contextual synthesis,
not creative generation.Specifically, AI identifies relevant verses from
verified scripture databases (Bible, Quran, Torah, Vedas, etc.) and
structures the response neutrally.AI is also used to generate text-to-
speech audio for scripture readings to improve accessibility.
Do you have the consent of owners of the works that were (or will be)
used to produce the AI generated portion of your projects? Please
explain.
Yes. CommonLight uses public-domain that are legally available for
educational and non-commercial use.Attribution and translation credits
are provided within the app and database.No copyrighted or private
data is used to generate outputs.
I am incorporating AI in my project in another way.
My project is not raising funds for AI tools themselves, nor does it use AI to
create new art or writing.AI is used only as an educational retrieval system
— to match user questions with relevant scripture passages and optionally
read them aloud through text-to-speech.The goal is to make authentic
religious texts more accessible, not to alter or reinterpret them.
