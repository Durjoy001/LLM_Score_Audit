Use of AI
My project is not raising funds for AI tools, nor am I using content that
was generated by AI. However, I will be using AI by incorporating it into
the core functionality of Taskclan. The AI system will optimize task
matching between users and freelance Runners, based on factors such
as location, availability, task complexity, and user preferences. This use
of AI will significantly enhance the efficiency and accuracy of the
platform, creating a seamless experience for both users and Runners.
