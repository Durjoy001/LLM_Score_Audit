About
Many people don't know their rights. Many organizations cannot keep up
with the explosion of new requirements for health and safety, especially
around workplace safety? In addition, there is both federal legislation that
applies to everybody as well as protections that vary from state/province to
state/province. For instance, did you know that in Ontario you now have the
right to an independent investigation of a complaint of harassment by a
trained professional (Bill 132)? Or that many definitions of workplace
violence include suspected domestic violence? Many states have legislation
that deals with specific high risk occupations (i.e. health care and retail). Did
you also know that organizations can be held liable for NOT doing a yearly
risk assessment?
SIMPLE COMPLIANCE software has been designed to provide organizations
with a comprehensive risk assessment, e-learning system and incident
reporting app that is monitored by an independent third party and held on
secure external servers to ensure complaints don't "get lost". The software
acts as an independent 3rd party ombudsman that ensures that people have
a safe workplace and will not be retaliated against for reporting incidences of
harassment/health and safety concerns.

WE NEED YOUR HELP TO CONTINUE!!!!!
We are raising funds for 3 uses:
1. We have developed an organizational risk assessment that we need the
validate. As part of the law in several provinces in Canada (including Ontrio
and British Columbia), a risk assessment must be completed yearly. We
have developed a comprehensive risk assessment that looks at 12 different
areas of the organization and assess them for workplace violence risks.
Once the risk assessment is validated at an academic level, we can offer it
as part of our overall package.
2. Updates to keep pace with the law. The law in this area is growing.
Recently there was Bill 138 in Ontario which states that everybody has the
right to an independent, trained investigator when an incident has been
submitted. We are updating our software to ensure that investigation reports
can be accessed by authorized personnel with security to ensure that it
cannot be changed once an investigator has submitted it. We are doing this
by generating an e-mail to all interested parties with a code that they can
access the report on a read only basis.
3. Assessment tools and resources to help ensure independent
investigations.
Risks and challenges
Liability is the largest risk we face. WE ARE NOT LAWYERS AND THE
SOFTWARE IS NOT INTENDED TO PROVIDE LEGAL ADVICE. It is simply
intended to give organizations and people the tools that they need to be
compliant with the law. We are providing this service as part of our
Kickstarter campaign to help individuals know their rights and how to access
our software to protect themselves from harassment and workplace
violence. In the event that further action is required, a lawyer may need to be
consulted.
Another challenge is that the laws are different in each state/province and
are constantly being changed or new laws added. We have to ensure our
software is constantly up-to-date to ensure compliance in as many
jurisdictions as possible.
