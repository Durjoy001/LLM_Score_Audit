The way in which candidates are applying for jobs, is changing.
The expectations of the prospective employer, are also changing. Mar 19 2018 - Apr 14 2018 (25
days)
We know that validating prior professional experience is critical in the
recruiting stages.

Steven D. Levitt, the co-author of Freakonomics, and an economics
professor at the University of Chicago cites research suggesting that more
than 50% of job applicants lie on their resumes.
Our Tool
Our tool will innovate the validation process of past employment and
professional experience. In addition, our tool is far more trusted, accurate
& interactive, than the previous process around professional validation.
Organizations can trust our tool to be valuable and meaningful, which is
critical in these stages of recruiting.
Benefits for Candidates:
- Eliminate the reactive approach for seeking professional validation
- Interactive
- EASY
- Fun
Benefits for Employers:
- Save 70% of time seeking professional validation on candidates
- Real time information
- Improve the value and accuracy in this process
- EASY
- Trusted

Being fully involved in the recruiting process, both from an HR perspective
and candidate, I realized that the way Recruiters/Hiring Managers interact
with candidates can be improved. We need to make it more personal and
customized to stay engaged with our talent.
In addition, there are several processes within the recruitment realm, that
delay decisions and simply waste time. One of which is the validation of
prior experience. My tool will be:
Proactive
Accurate
Easy to use
Interactive
