ADHD, so it's hard for me to concentrate, and I always have gaps in my Sep 28 2022 - Oct 28 2022 (30

am. So that's why I created this software. This software is designed to
help students learn by asking questions and getting explanations back.
Imagine being able to learn by just asking questions and getting answers
back. That's what this software does. Any question you have, any topic
you're struggling with, you can get help with. I'm raising funds to help
develop this software, hire a team, and use funds to train a better machine
learning model. This is very costly since you need much computer
processing power to train a machine learning model. I believe this software
will help change how people learn and make it easier for them to get the
education they deserve. I am willing to put in the extra work to make this
software the best it can be, and I'm asking for your help. I appreciate your
support.
