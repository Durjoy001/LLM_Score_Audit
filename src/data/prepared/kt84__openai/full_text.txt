There have been and still are many attempts to build a software that can
imitate human brain. Nov 25 2014 - Jan 24 2015 (60
days)
Obviously this is a very large scale project that can't be easily planned and
meets a lot of roadblocks.

I think what's being missed here is that human brain itself, as we know it
now, has gone through stages of evolution.
Discovering, learning and evolving as a human brain took time and
happened in stages.
Other than bureaucracy, lack of clear vision etc., setting an
overwhelmingly high goal doomed such projects up for a failure.
My plan is simple and achievable and can be further expanded, in
reasonable steps.
I believe 2 excellent developers, myself, a part time neuroscience expert
and a part time mathematician can achieve building a virtual intelligence in
less than 2 years that can understand human input (English sentence to
start), can infer and learn from it, respond in a humanly manner. I would
need 3 months to put an outstanding team together and get started.
While making money is not the short-term goal that drives this project,
there is a huge potential for this intelligent software to add power to
virtually any domain.
I am looking for investors who's target is not making easy money. Science
enthusiasts who believe it's time for humanity to push boundaries!
