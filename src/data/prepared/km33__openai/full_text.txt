FEATURES
ALL CREDIT CARD TYPES SUPPORTED
Ability to add an unlimited number of companies. Support for Multiple
Credit Cards (Visa, MasterCard, Amex or many of each).
EVERY BANK WORLDWIDE
Multiple Sets of Credit Cards, General Ledger Accounts and Payees.
Mostly used Sales Taxes already configured. New ones easily added. Past
and Present Transaction Reports can be printed and archived.
SAFETY AND CONFIDENTIALITY
Our corporation and servers are located in Montreal, Quebec, Canada.
Latest technology used to ensure Maximum Safety and Confidentiality.
Your data can be deleted at any time.
Management Plus is smart and intuitive. It learns your specific needs and
works faster and better every time you use it.
HOW IT WORKS
REGISTER
Login to your bank account online and Download your Credit Card
Statement in .CSV Format.
GET LOGIN DETAILS
Login to your Private and Confidential Management Plus account and
upload the .CSV file to the application
VIEW AND PRINT REPORT
Print. Save. Archive. Even come back later to consult previous statements.
