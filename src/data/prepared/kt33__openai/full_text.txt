Use of AI
FaceTheCamera is a human-designed app that helps people look
confident and natural on camera. Our team created all product
concepts, UX flows, and interface designs manually. We use AI and
open-source technologies only to enhance video quality and user
experience — never to generate creative content or marketing
materials.AI and Open-Source Components Used in the Product
Teleprompter Integration: The teleprompter system is built using
integrated open-source React teleprompter components. Our team
customized the interface, scrolling behavior, and overlay controls to
ensure smooth reading and on-screen usability. This module does not
rely on AI — it is powered entirely by standard front-end technology.
Smart Eye Contact & Head Stabilization: To help users maintain natural
eye contact and camera presence, we use Microsoft Azure Video APIs
and NVIDIA Maxine for real-time face tracking and head stabilization.
These AI tools analyze visual data to subtly stabilize gaze and head
movement. They do not replace, alter, or generate any part of the userʼs
face or expressions.FluentVoice (Filler Word Removal): This module
uses the OpenAI Realtime API and Whisper Flow for real-time speech
recognition and cleanup. AI detects filler words such as “um” or “uh”
and regenerates smoother audio based on the userʼs original voice
recording. The output remains 100% human — AI functions only as a
clarity and fluency filter.Expression Relaxation AI: Powered by NVIDIA
Maxine, this optional feature gently reduces facial tension (e.g., nervous
blinking or stiffness) to help users appear calm and confident.
Adjustments are subtle and occur in real time; the system never
generates or replaces faces.AI Use in Marketing or Creative Materials:

narration was created using AI text-to-speech solely for demonstration
purposes. No AI was used to create or modify human likenesses in the
video.Human Contribution: All app concepts, UX/UI designs, feature
