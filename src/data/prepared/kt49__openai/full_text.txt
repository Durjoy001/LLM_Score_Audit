Why The Fuss
Over the past decade the online share of total worldwide retail sales has
steadily been growing, reaching well over $4 trillion in 2020.
Unfortunately, the other side of the coin is a continuously growing number
of fraud attacks - global losses from payment fraud has tripled from $9.84
billion in 2011 to $32.39 in 2020, of which the United States claims more
than one third.
The unprecedented jump in payment fraud weʼve seen since the onset of
global pandemic is attributed not only to a drastic increase in e-commerce
activity, but also to the fact that in all times fraudsters take advantage of
confusion, uncertainty and vulnerability caused by COVID-19 crisis. As
both businesses and consumers are struggling to stay afloat due to a
decline in the global economy, fraudsters are targeting them more
aggressively than ever. A recent TransUnion survey found that 22% of
Americans have been targeted by fraud scams related to COVID-19. A new
study from Juniper Research forecasts that businesses in eCommerce,
airline ticketing, money transfer and banking services, will cumulatively
lose over $200 billion to online payment fraud between 2020 and 2024.
Fraudsters are constantly developing new schemes and using advanced
technologies to be at the top of their game. The only way for merchants to
combat this is to invest in the right risk management solution.

Our Product At A Glance
The HawkEye Platform – payment fraud detection engine, which delivers
risk mitigating decisions in real-time. It incorporates multiple AI tools and
decision layers to analyze transaction flows in a holistic way and unlock
hidden insights in fraudulent behaviors.
Layer 1: HawkEye Rule Engine
The Rules Engine is the first line of defense. It captures a high amount of
fraud by screening transactional data against a set of rules derived from
known fraud patterns. Our rules monitor various dimensions pertaining to
payments, such as payment amount, frequency of declined authorization
attempts, pattern of duplicated card numbers, number of refunds, etc.
Using them we detect suspicious transactions.
Layer 2: HawkEye Supervised Machine Learning Model
In the second layer, we use a fully connected Deep Neural Network (DNN)
to classify a transaction as fraud or not fraud. To do this, we first use
merchantʼs historical data - where each transaction is labeled as either
fraud or non-fraud - to train a supervised learning model to classify
transactions. Then, we apply this model on top of the existing rules engine
to distinguish the fraudulent transactions that may have not been alerted
by the rules.
Layer 3: HawkEye Autoencoder
Here we attempt to uncover some cases of fraud that got past the Rules
Engine and Supervised Model. Our Autoencoder is trained to create
feature representation of a valid transaction so it can effectively detect
deviations from the norm, even if such deviations are very subtle and only
noticeable when considering a series of events and seemingly
uncorrelated features. If a deviation is above some pre-set threshold it is
considered a candidate for fraud.
Clients can connect to HawkEye Platform using REST API so the decisions
can be easily integrated into their information systems.
