Drawing inspiration from beloved classics such as Deus Ex and System
Shock, Incoherence is an immersive simulation set within the enigmatic
confines of an abandoned complex. In this game, you'll delve into a
world where exploration is key. Scour for hidden items that reveal the
way forward and allow you to craft useful resources. Gain experience
through your actions and unlock an array of skills and weapon
upgrades, enhancing your ability to navigate the complex's many
challenges. Master the art of controlling electronic devices, giving you a
crucial advantage against the enigmatic inhabitants of the complex.
As you explore, the story of the megacorporation unravels through a
rich blend of environmental storytelling and interactions with NPCs.
Engage in conversations with the remnants of the corporation's
employees, each character offering unique insights and pieces of the
puzzle. Discover the narrative layers hidden in notes and emails left
behind, piecing together the events that led to the corporation's
downfall.
Immerse yourself in a first-person adventure, rich in detail and
atmosphere.
Equip a variety of classic weapons, each with unique strengths and
abilities.
Interact with complex NPCs, adding depth to the unfolding story.
Encounter diverse monsters, testing your combat skills and
strategies.
Evolve your character through RPG elements, with leveling and skill
trees.
Tailor your arsenal with weapon upgrades to suit your playstyle.
Unearth blueprints and use found materials to craft vital resources.
Explore computer systems and find notes that piece together the
game's narrative and open new pathways.
Overcome challenges by repairing and hacking robots and security
systems.
Collect in-game currency scattered throughout the world for use at
various vendors.

Risks and challenges
While developing a unique and creative digital project like ours, it's not
uncommon for timelines to shift and unforeseen obstacles to emerge.
One of the primary challenges we anticipate is evolving our game in
response to community feedback. The nature and volume of feedback
could potentially lead to adjustments in our development milestones.
Should there be any changes to our timeline, rest assured, we'll keep
our backers and community fully informed.
Additionally, we're exploring the integration of advanced features like
DLSS. However, this is contingent on securing a licensing agreement. If
we're unable to finalize this aspect, it might affect the final game
features.
This Kickstarter campaign is crucial for us to expand our team and stay
committed to delivering a game that meets and exceeds expectations,
navigating through these challenges. Our priority remains firmly on
crafting an exceptional gaming experience for our players.
