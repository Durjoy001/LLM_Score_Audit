Story

Funding period
NetWatcher is a creative application designed to provide surveillance over
Internet on specified computer and tablet devices. All screens of Jan 7 2016 - Feb 26 2016 (50
monitored devices are monitored, so if you attach a video camera to your days)
computer or tablet, the surrounding environment can be monitored
remotely from any corner of the world.

More details can be found at http://www.netguarder.com/
NetWatcher originally emerged into market in 2003 via NetGurader and
holds copyrights authorized by National Copyright Administration of the
People's Republic of China, it was limited to monitor local network
connected computers. Currently the completely new designed NetWatcher
makes a breakthrough in network boundary and gets involved into modern
life with network everywhere.
We have developed the whole version on Windows platforms. On each
popular tablet and mobile platform, we need develop a NetWatcher agent,
our project here is to collect money to develop a version on Android
platform only and make a small market promotion if it is possible.
For Families
Parents can use NetWatcher to observe and control children's computer
usage and internet surfing in very close and secret manner. It's a powerful
deterrent force for kids' better behaviors.
Families can also connect extra video cameras to NetWatcher agent
enabled devices and build up a remote surveillance system easily.
For Enterprises
Many companies get to know many employees work with a little effort for
real tasks, while they spend a lot of energy on surfing Internet in working
time. According to American PMI (Projects Management Institute) 2003
report, 75% companies take measures on network surveillance in order to
enhance enterprise work efficiency. NetWatcher is such a kind of
supervision solution, designed for managers and technical people.
