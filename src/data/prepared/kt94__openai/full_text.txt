Risks and challenges
Marshmallow Studios is a startup and, as such, there are always risks and
challenges associated with starting a new business. Some of the key risks
and challenges include:
-Developing a platform that is easy to use and meets the needs of both
instructors and users
-Building a user base for the platform
-Developing relationships with instructors and getting them on board with
the platform
-Competing with other platforms that offer similar services
The challenges, however, won't affect the mission to connect individuals to
instructors, all over the world, making it easy for anyone to learn any active
sport they want without having to take unnecessary risks.
Our absence alone... is a risk.
