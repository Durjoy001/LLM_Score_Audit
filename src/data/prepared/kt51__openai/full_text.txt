👥 2. Scaling for Teams and Organizations
Supporting hundreds (and eventually thousands) of teams will require
strong backend infrastructure and customer support.
How weʼll overcome it:
Weʼre already set up on scalable cloud infrastructure. Kickstarter funds will
go directly toward hiring extra support and optimizing systems for
performance and stability.
3. Feature Scope Creep
Thereʼs always a risk of trying to do too much at once, especially with
feedback from early users.
How weʼll overcome it:
Weʼve defined a clear roadmap for our 1.0 release and will stay laser-
focused on delivering the core tools first: scheduling, chat, RSVP,
registration, and admin dashboards. All other requests will be prioritized
transparently after launch.
💸 4. Sustaining the Business Long-Term
Building the platform is one thing — maintaining and supporting it long-
term is another.
How weʼll overcome it:
Weʼve built in a subscription model for sustainability (powered by
RevenueCat), and weʼre targeting partnerships with leagues, clubs, and
sports organizations. Kickstarter gives us the momentum to build a solid
base - and weʼre in this for the long haul.
In short, we know this wonʼt be easy - but with over 8 years of coaching
