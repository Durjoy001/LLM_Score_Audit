auditions, faster. It finds the right roles across major casting platforms, Dec 11 2025 - Jan 10 2026 (30

automatically—saving hours of work every day. Whether you’re a

edge and puts your career on fast-forward.

Back this campaign to be among the first to access CastAI and see how
effortless auditioning can become. Early supporters will get exclusive

Why CastAI Matters
Actors spend countless hours searching for roles, preparing submissions,
and competing against hundreds or thousands of others, all while trying to
improve their craft. CastAI removes the tedious, repetitive work so you can
focus on acting, training, and performing. It levels the playing field, giving
every actor, especially newcomers and independents, access to
opportunities they might otherwise miss.

Role Discovery & Matching: Instantly find auditions tailored to your profile
