Risks and challenges
1. Limited Reach: iMessage chatbots can only be accessed by users
who have iPhones or other Apple devices. This can limit the potential
audience for your chatbot service.
-> The team has future milestones to bring this service to Android users
in the future.
2. User Privacy Concerns: iMessage users may hesitate to share
personal information to get the most optimized answer due to privacy
concerns.
-> The team is working to ensure the chatbot is compliant with privacy
regulations (GDPR and CCPA).
3. Apple's Approval Process: Apple has a strict approval process for
iMessage apps and chatbots.
-> The team has already gotten Apple's approval and meets all
guidelines.
4. Difficulty in Discoverability: Unless iMessage users know about our
chatbot, they won't be able to use it. -> The team is working religiously
to promote the chatbot and get users to engage with it.
