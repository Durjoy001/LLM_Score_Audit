Refutal is a free political debate and discussion platform. Itʼs free for users
to participate in debates, and free to run your own basic guild of up to 30
members. Users who wish to expand their guildʼs member base or want to 
experience every feature we plan to offer can upgrade for a low monthly
rate.
The project team behind Refutal has grown more and more frustrated with
today's social media and technology in general. Everywhere you look, from
Facebook to Twitter to Google platforms and Discord, the ADL has
partnered or invested, and fundamentally changed the rules for what can
and cannot be discussed. Companies like Blizzard, Riot Games, and others
have taken principal investments from the Chinese company Tencent and
similarly limited the capacity for free speech.
And we're sick of it!
Refutal seeks to change this paradigm by offering a true free speech
platform for political debate. We want to offer a platform for everyone to
speak their mind no matter how controversial or politically upsetting their
opinions may be. (See: The Hong-Kongtroversy regarding Grandmaster
Hearthstone player, "Chung Ng Wai" being banned for advocating
democracy in Hong-Kong).
My plan
We have set up our website and are working diligently to create a beta
version of the client by the end of December of this year; complete with
competitive and unique features, such as political profiling; our custom AI-
moderation bots and command bots for server owners.
