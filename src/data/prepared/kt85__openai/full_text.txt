be a costly mistake for business owners. This is what motivated the team
at Reason Square to tackle inefficiencies that sucks away close to $150
billion of the global economy. The term coined by LinkedIn in a survey
conducted through an interaction of over 277 million professionals, paired
with a PwC database of 2,000 employees. (Also Using Algorithms we
presume…) is talent mismatch. Talent mismatch occurs when a candidate
ideally suited for an organization, who typically has passed the strict
internal controls, interview procedures, induction processes and training,
simply does not deliver what the employer expected.
In spite of these rigorous procedures, taken both by staffing agencies, who
are paid a lucrative fee to conduct their searches, and employers
themselves, talent mismatch is proving to an opportunity cost of a
significant standing.
The most significant loss occurs at the productivity level, which was
quantified by LinkedIn at being $130 billion. The research quotes the
following: “The research found a strong correlation between the
adaptability of the talent in a particular country and the performance of its
companies. If markets were better at matching talent with the right
opportunities, this could unlock as much as USD$130 billion of productivity
in the 11 markets studied (including USD$65.6 billion in China, USD$29.3
billion in the US and USD$11.7 billion in Brazil). Avoidable recruitment costs
of USD$19.8bn2 –this lack of access to the right talent is driving up the
cost of recruitment for employers today. The longer time taken to find the
right candidates, and the increased likelihood for mismatched talent to
leave sooner are costing companies in those countries USD$19.8bn in
avoidable recruitment costs.”

The premise behind our software, was to find the answers to the following
challenging questions
• What can be done to lower this significant opportunity cost • How can
we use modern technological tools to alleviate this burden looming over
companies that have yet to shown immunity, irrespective of their size,
geographical location and industry.
We hoped to develop a platform, which centers on the following principles:
➢ Preventing Talent Mismatch within organizations ➢ Providing a software
tool capable of doing much more than a simple Applicant Tracking System
(ATS) would ➢ Keeping in mind the productivity and revenue measures of
our clients and ensuring that we provided them the tools to help better
manage their organization ➢ Giving our clients a competitive edge, driven
by the sophistication of the software and its ability to solve complex
recruitment problems ➢ A software which not only tracks applicants, but
also provides strategic guidance ➢ Can offer a holistic, modular approach
towards recruiting which includes every key element of the value chain.
Hired Square is our technological response to the challenges recruitment
agencies face on a daily basis.

business performance and increasing profitability
• Integrating with potential partners, modular platform which can
seamlessly fit into existing software and techniques.
• Enhancing existing within ATS and HR Management technology with a
value added approach
