
Why We’re Building Near22
Most chat apps demand your phone number, an account, and endless
tracking. Even for something simple like saying hello to someone nearby,
youʼre forced to hand over your identity.
We believe connecting in the real world shouldnʼt come at the cost of your
privacy. Thatʼs why weʼre building Near22 — a privacy-first chat app
where you can discover and talk to people nearby instantly, with no logins,
no accounts, and no tracking.
What We’ve Built So Far
✅ iOS TestFlight beta is already live
✅ Nearby discovery via Bluetooth
✅ Groups, contacts, and chat working in dark and light mode
✅ Push notifications (APNs) functioning
🚧 Android beta in progress

What We’re Raising Funds to Do
With your support, we will:
Launch the public iOS app on the App Store
Release the Android beta
Add advanced privacy features like local-only queues and BLE
optimizations
Explore small business use cases (private groups, BYOD ordering)
Our goal is to keep Near22 independent — supported by backers, not
advertisers or data brokers.
How We’ll Make It Happen
Weʼve already developed the core app and infrastructure. With
Kickstarter funding, we can:
Cover hosting and scaling costs
Finalize iOS App Store release
Accelerate Android development
Grow an early community of privacy-first supporters
Who We Are
👋 I’m Siva Boju, founder and developer of Near22. Iʼve been building
mobile apps and backend systems for years, and Iʼm passionate about
creating a space where people can connect without giving up their privacy.
Iʼve been working solo on Near22, from design to code to infrastructure,
and your support on Kickstarter helps make this vision real.
Risks and challenges
App Store review timelines may cause delays.
Bluetooth behavior differs across devices and requires tuning.
Scaling backend services depends on community support.
But unlike many projects, Near22 already has a working beta — so the risk
of “never shipping” is low.
