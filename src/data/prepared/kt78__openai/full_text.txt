Funding period
We are a student run tutoring company. What we do is provide university
students with the opportunity to tutor in order to help fund their education. Sep 10 2014 - Oct 13 2014 (33
days)
We work in the USA and Canada!
University costs in Canada have risen to ABOVE $10,000 per year in Tuition

ALONE!!! Students need access to flexible jobs that help them make
money while attending school.
As we grew, it became difficult to manage students and tutor matches.
Last year we spend $4,000 building a software, however we couldn't
afford someone locally, and that's all the money we had, so we were
forced to outsource to India. We got scammed, the company we hired
gave us the runaround, refused to listen to our instructions, took four times
longer than they told us, did almost no work, and we were given a piece of
junk that is unusable.
We have found a local programmer who can build us the program we
need. It will help optimize tutor/student matches, and be able to give more
work to University students and improve efficiency.
By pledging our project you are helping with 3 goals:
1. Provide work to a local programmer.
2. Provide jobs for university students needing work.
3. Provide affordable tutoring for students/families who need it.
Risks and challenges
Our software was already planned, but a company in India scammed us.
Took our money, and gave us junk. However the situation gave us the
opportunity to learn what to avoid in the future. We have since planned and
fine tuned all the specifications. We have found a local programmer that
has references and provided samples of his work.
