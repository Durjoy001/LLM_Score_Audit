The idea came as I was looking for some help around my backyard. I
needed someone, to help me cut the trees in preparation for winter. Ideally, Nov 10 2020 - Jan 9 2021 (60
I needed that help now or the following day. I was thinking - where can I days)
find this person? How much would it cost me?...
Then the idea was born.. just like you can rent a place to stay, where and

when you want, just like you can call on services to deliver your food from
a restaurant of your liking at the time and location you want, why can't you
use an app to find services around you - plumber, electrician, gardener,
handyman, tow truck, painter... anything you want, at the time you needed.
See what's available around you, and hire. The professionals would have a
rating, and also provide their availabilities. My platform would allow you to
chat, privately, and, if you are happy, book the service. The rate would be
there, no hidden fees, no surprises... just like when you book an airbnb or
ubereat.
The project is also great for local businesses... Say you have a few workers
available for a few days or hours, just use my platform to create yourself
visibility.
