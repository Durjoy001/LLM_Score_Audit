WHO WE ARE
Julian Tinoco is the fonder of Lacetech. He is a Software Developer
specialized in Test Automation. He worked in different companies such as;
Accedian Networks, Caterpillars, AGT Robotics, Newtrax and Broadsign.
Having the chance to work in different technologies, from
Telecomunications, Robotics to Embedded System (Hardware) and Web.
Julian started Test Automation projects from scratch, and he observed that
the success of Test Automation projects is the balance between ROI
(Return On Investment), Technology and of course Team Work. One of
Julian's biggest goal is the reduction of 25% of manual tests that represent
three weeks of software testing.
Julian started a Cloud Automation Test software, using GNU General
Public License. During this process, he wondered, why Web Designers
spend about 30%-40% of their time, checking the responsive of their
websites? This is the reason why OLA was created.
The Lacetech's team is growing up faster and they are located in different
countries, such as; Canada, Australia and Colombia.
FAQ
What a OLA minute represents?
This is the time spent by each computer. When OLA is executed, OLA
defines how many computers are needed, in order to have a report in few
minutes. To give you an idea, a normal website with 10 pages, 10 different
resolutions and two (2) browsers, provide a report in less than 5 minutes.
Can I download the software and use OLA in my own?
Yes, but keep in mind:
Lacetech works with the version of the software for 6 months to assure
his stability, after it the code is launched for the public.
If you run OLA in your laptop, it could take more than five (5) times if
you run OLA through our platform.
