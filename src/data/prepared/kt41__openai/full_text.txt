The AR Game Where Your World Becomes the Stage
What if your local mall, park, or city streets suddenly came alive with
heroes, monsters, and wild creatures from another world? 
In Ryftborne: Shadows of the Void, mysterious tears — called Ryfts — are
breaking open again. Years ago, the first Ryft spilled life from a strange
dimension known as the Void. Most people forgot it ever happened.
But the Ryfts are back. Wider. Stronger. Impossible to ignore.
From towering beasts to legendary heroes, these Ryftborne beings arenʼt
waiting for us to step into their world — theyʼre crossing into ours.
🌌 The Void: Where the Shadows Begin
Each Ryft is more than a tear in the sky — itʼs a breach that allows its
creatures to spill into our cities, our homes, our world. Some come seeking
allies. Others… hunger for chaos.
And now, they walk among us — visible only through the lens of your
device.
The question isnʼt whether youʼll see them.
Itʼs whether youʼll stop them.
