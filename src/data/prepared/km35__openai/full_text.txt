Why Typical Online Travel Booking Websites Fail?
The main disadvantages of online travel booking these days all come down
to the lack of custom personalization and inefficient travel booking
experiences.
According to a study made by Expedia in 2015, travelers visit on average
38 sites before booking. 38!
Do you know why these people have to search so many travel booking
websites?
Because there are way too many websites to visit out there and no
complete solution that can match the exact needs and desires that they are
looking for when it comes to the travel experience they want the very
most. It's really that simple.
That’s pretty frustrating, isn't it? We're sure you can relate.
But luckily for all of us, those endless searches and frustrations are about
to become a thing of the past. My experienced team and I are going to
forever change the way you ever search for travel deals ever again. And
you're going to be blown away by it.
No need to spend days trying to find "The Perfect Travel Deal" anymore
online. We're about to make it fast, simple and fully customized to YOU.
Let's face the real truth for a minute...
Planning and booking that most perfect trip can become complicated and
frustrating very quickly.
Understanding what a traveler really wants and needs is still at the very
heart of the person in question.
When you use Uvergo to plan and book your own trips, you'll be working
with highly intuitive artificial intelligence (AI), that will fully understand
what you need in a way that no travel agent or actual website can.
