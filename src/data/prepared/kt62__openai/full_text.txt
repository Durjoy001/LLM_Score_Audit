What I'm Building
LobbyBot is a desktop app for windows that lets players customize their in-
game music for popular titles such as Fortnite and Valorant. It syncs
seamlessly with your system and replaces default lobby tunes with your
own custom audio — timed perfectly with the game's menu flow. Think
Spotify vibes, but built specifically for gaming immersion.
I am raising funds to take LobbyBot from polished prototype to full public
launch — with a sleek UI, expanded features, better backend stability, and
support for more games. The core tech is already built and working — I
just need your help to level it up!
