So What's The Problem Here?
We get most of our daily information and news from our social media
platforms. Weʼve become accustomed to wasting countless hours through
a form of escapism, and in large part control very little of what we see on
our platforms. Whatʼs worse is that people get most of their news from a
social media platform that have likely sourced its information from heavily
biased/ illegitimate news sources or from popular pages with large
followings who often propagate fake or unverified information. From there,
the information is molested within the climate that is social media and
turned into memes, trends, challenges, and jokes. Not only are sources of
information in large part inaccessible to people, but this hierarchy and
centralization of information presents us with numerous challenges that
handicaps our “democracy of information” and limits our society to focus
only on what is given to us with no other sources to get reliable news
from.

Popular news corporations and information conglomerates have biases.
Itʼs a problem we all know exists and until now, we have just had to accept
it. Whether itʼs the personal biases of reporters, the ideologies of the
people who fund them, or the numerous 3rd party institutions who oversee
it all, we see this issue in our news every day. Time after time, news is
spun to fit the narrative that the broadcaster wants, often polarizing issues
based on ideological preference . All too often, key issues are not reported
on or are put on the back-burner never to be addressed or heard because
they either do not fit the existing dialogue or simply because there is only
so much that can be aired at any one time. Rather than stubbornly cling to
the constricting nature of a centralized news structure that limits the free
flow of information, our world should be shared collectively by the very
people experiencing it! To put the spread of news and information in our
hands!
So What Are We Doing About It?
Introducing InSite, the revolutionary News Sharing app designed to
provide news and information first hand by people just like you and I! The
aim of our platform is to remove bias and censorship, in order to give
communities control over what they see and what gets reported on.
Whether in Toronto, London, Mexico City, or Tokyo. A location based
verification system will allow users to “upvote” or “downvote” posts based
on eye-witness accounts of events globally, letting users decide, what is
most important to them. Connecting you, your community and the world,
while leaving the power of news and information in your hands!

So How Does This All Work?
The main interface of the app contains a map of the user's position, and
shows nearby posts, allowing users to see what is going on in their
community. This also allows users to navigate to the location of an event
and verify its truthfulness. Users may also navigate between videos,
categorized into two sections; Happening Near Me and Trending
Worldwide. The Happening Near Me tab takes the user to the closest
“trending” posts to them, and allows users to scroll through videos, sorted
by proximity. The Trending Worldwide tab takes the user to posts with the
most engagement and significance based on community consensus
across the entire globe, allowing users to scroll through posts sorted by
view count and location.
On a post, users are presented with a full screen video that scales to the
size of your device. Along with the video, users have several items that

the news that matters. With dynamically scaling cloud servers and
databases, as well as a three-day lifespan for posts that lack engagement,
the download and upload of information will never be slow and additionally,
can never be falsified as information must go through the app and cannot
be downloaded or uploaded externally, ensuring both geolocation and time
stamps for videos to always be referenced back to. InSite also offers an
anonymous mode, that enables users to post without their information
available to other users. These measures ensure that news maintain
credibility and integrity, untouched by individual biases or malicious intent.

So You'reSayingI Can Get Paid For This?
InSite has a built-in cryptocurrency reward system, where users are paid*
to verify, interact with, and post information. When a user creates a new
post, and as it gets more views and more verifications, the poster is
rewarded for engagement they generate. Similarly, as a userʼs Rep
increases, so will their pay, and any interaction with the app including
posting and verifying of any sort, including videos and articles will impact
this Rep and therefore affect the reward amount, incentivizing users to
post reputable and factual news for the benefit of their community.
With Your Help, Here Is What You Can ExpectNext!
As we continue to build and improve InSite and its ecosystem, we will add
more categories to refine what news you want to see. We also will have
machine-learning algorithms, that will build you a stream of posts that you
both find interesting and at times, can challenge your existing views to give
you an unbiased lens on any story.
InSite currently has a completely working prototype and, with your help,
we can turn that prototype into a beautiful and seamless user experience
that does away with bias, censorship, and can in-fact support big media
for OUR needs. We plan to release a Sister app that goes along with InSite
that allows journalists to compose and create news stories with
accompanying articles and sources in order to share a more cultivated
stream of news that users can choose to interact with. This journalist
program will pay journalists the same way: for engagement and honest
media. It will act as source for humanitarians to build awareness, for
freelance journalists to tell a story and for regular people like you and me
to inform ourselves of things we otherwise wouldnʼt know or even think
about both in our communities and throughout the world. Until now, social
media has done a fabulous job of making the individual social. What InSite
aims to do, is to make news social. We all need some InSite!!
