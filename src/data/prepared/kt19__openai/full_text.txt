So goes the saying: "Paper can take anything!", and consumer agreements
do. Not all agreements are logically sound and may not translate into
logically consistent structures. In such cases, we intend to work with
vendors towards resolution, case by case.
Legally binding terms may not have single interpretation. Different experts
my interpret same terms in a different way. This is why we have courts. A
significant feature of our technology is ability to introduce a new variable
and, when needed, offer a choice of interpretation.
Scalability! Supporting a network of millions is a fundamentally different
challenge of supporting a network of thousands. We have already touched
upon this issue in a "Scenario If Project Overfunded". However, if it turns
out that number of backers approach the limits of our technology,
scalability will be addressed with high priority.
