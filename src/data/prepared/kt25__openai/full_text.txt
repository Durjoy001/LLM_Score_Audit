On joining Serendipity, you begin having four-minute text-chat
conversations with other members, who are adults of any age from
practically everywhere on Earth. After each conversation, you and your
new acquaintance rank each other on multiple instances of three
categories of personal identifiers: appearance, individuality, and character.
As you have more conversations, a profile emerges of how others perceive
you; the more Serendipity conversations you have, the clearer your profile
becomes. You can rely on Serendipity being a true mirror of your image

Given that the Serendipity app accumulates and compiles detailed
information about how others perceive you, you may wonder how safe the
data concerning you is with us. The answer: perfectly safe. Each
Serendipity user is absolutely anonymous to all other Serendipity users.
You will identify any other user only through a pseudonym, which is how
any other user will identify you. Aside from your registration information,
you are even anonymous to the Serendipity app itself!
For a long time weʼve been interested in how the strength of first
impressions combines with the concept of perceived value—read books
about these ideas, and followed and contributed to blogs. But when we
read James Surowieckiʼs The Wisdom of the Crowds: Why the Many Are
Smarter Than the Few and How Collective Wisdom Shapes Business,
Economies, Societies and Nations, the light bulb really lit up. As social-
media programmers, we saw how we could connect all three of these
ideas, and help people connect with each other in a more positive way. We
saw how social media could overcome the errors inherent in first
impressions and perceived value. We designed Serendipity for scalability,
including the eventual integration with mobile and other applications.
