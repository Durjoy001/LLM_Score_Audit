Don't have time to go to the yoga studio? Away on a business trip? Don't
like to work out in a group environment? Stay healthy with yoga. Feb 12 2016 - Mar 13 2016 (30
days)
yogagym is the online yoga service that allows users to access yoga
classes anytime, anywhere. Sign up for a silver or gold subscription and
get access to yoga instructors at home, in your hotel or on the beach -

wherever you want, whenever you want.
yogagym provides 2 levels of yoga training - beginners and advanced. The
instructors will provide advice and explanations in the video sessions to
give the users the best possible guidance to perform the exercises correct.
There will be a chat room and message service with access to online help
and yoga instructors to answer questions.
The website and mobile app will be hosted on the Azure platform and the
framework will be develop locally. We will use certified experienced yoga
instructors to create the educational content and exercises and all content
will be recorded in high definition to provide the best user experience.
There is plenty of possibility to add additional features as live sessions,
private sessions, accessories, nutrition and more.
Risks and challenges
The project can be divided into 3 phases.
The first phase is to develop the website and mobile app. The challenge
will be to stay within budget and on time. To manage the risk we'll have
weekly meetings with the developers to complete milestones and evaluate
user interface.
The second phase is to develop the content for the applications. The
challenge will be to develop the content in a way that is useful and user
friendly to the end user. That means the sound and video quality has to be
