<TL DR>
We prioritize privacy, allowing users to share personal or confidential
messages without fear of leaks or judgment. Conversations are securely 
locked between sender and receiver, preventing screenshots and
forwards. Unlike other messaging apps, Khat is designed for meaningful,
private exchanges, mimicking the experience of letter writing. Key features
include messages that self-delete after reading, acceptance codes to
ensure only the intended recipient can read the message, and AI-driven
spam protection. With a focus on emotional security, Khat provides a
trusted platform for users as young as 12 to communicate openly.
</TL DR>

A lot of people are asking how is it going to be different than other similar
apps that currently exist. Our absolute answer is , it's experience of use
will be different, it's purpose will be different. It's not for simple and short
conversations like "hey, how are you?" but it would be for little longer
conversations that pan out between 2 individuals and its existence will
forever remain between those two, and that is the guarantee we want to
provide.
The world does not need another whatsapp, or another telegram, we are
well aware of that, But what the world we believe really need is an app that
can act as your "own personal hotline". Handing over the user id to
selected few people means that you truly trust the person, and would like
to talk.
To achieve this - We are taking humanity 2 steps back to the age of letter
writing. We want to normalize letter writing.
Just like a letter, it has a writer, that is the sender, and a recipient of the
letter. The letter goes inside an envelope, and gets sealed, and stamped,
for delivering it to the right person. The recipient has a mailbox, where
letters come and sit - just like an email. However, there are features within
the app that concentrate on your privacy.
The sender can select "delete after reading". Letter gets deleted after it
has been read, from the servers, and recipients mailbox. Will not be
present in the sent items.
The sender can opt for "acceptance code". This is a key feature of the
app. If a letter is marked for acceptance code, then only the intended
recipient can open the letter, and no one else. Failing to open it more
than 3 times, will erase the letter.
Users on the application can be people as young as 12-13 years old. We
believe that kids are afraid to convey a lot of things that goes on in their
lives. With the help of this app, and the designed policy they can be
open about their feelings in front of their parents, teachers and other
mentors.
Only one recipient. No multiple recipients for the letter.
The Letter cannot be forwarded.
Letters cannot be taken screenshot. This is a SECURED feature of the
app. So that you can send confidential and personal information.
Users are identified using a username on the app, and not the first and
last name. That also acts as the mailbox address.
No signups anywhere using the username.
You can add attachments, and depending upon the size of the
attachment you would need to use appropriate envelope and stationery
for the letter.
