classes and objects to form larger and more complex structures.
☑ Behavioral Patterns: This family of design patterns such as Observer,
Strategy, Command, Chain of Responsibility, and State, address how
objects interact and communicate with each other.
☑ Architectural Patterns: We may also delve into architectural patterns
like Model-View-Controller (MVC) and Model-View-ViewModel (MVVM),
which define the overall structure and organization of an application.
☑ Concurrency Patterns: These patterns, like Thread Pool, Producer-
Consumer, and Active Object, focus on managing concurrent and parallel
execution in multi-threaded applications.
☑ Anti-Patterns: You'll gain an understanding of common design pitfalls
and anti-patterns to avoid in your software development process.
