After beating a Video Game, you can gain the ability to read & Musical
Notes?
Yes! "NoteCaptor" helps you memorize most common notes on both
treble clef and bass clef. It's a great tool for people who want to learn
music, especially beneficial for those learning to play the piano. You don't
need any prior knowledge or experience; it's beginner-friendly!
Where did the idea for "NoteCaptor" come from?
When I was learning music theory, I encountered various challenges.
Reading sheet music proved to be a struggle. As a beginner, I hadn't found
any interesting apps or games to make this process enjoyable. That's
when I came up with the idea: create a game that makes it easy and fun for
everyone to read musical notes.
