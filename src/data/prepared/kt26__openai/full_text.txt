Risks and challenges
We have cultivated a high quality diagnostic software which is built to be
long lasting and most importantly save our customers' money, so we
wouldn't want them to have to purchase something that wouldn't last!
Therefore we source our hardware from reliable manufacturers, so that the
device is guaranteed to work in optimal condition alongside the Carnostic
app. Of course there are always safety precautions to be taken when
working with any vehicle, but not to worry, all Carnostic devices come
equipped with detailed safety instructions on using the OBD device. For
further assistance and assurance we have demo videos available, outlining
the easy steps from start to finish! As always we guarantee to be
transparent with our backers and make their Carnostic journey smooth
sailing!
