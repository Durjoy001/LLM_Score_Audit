The New Funeral Product
May 22 2025 - Jul 4 2025 (43
days)
Today, a large majority of North Americans are choosing cremation over a
burial. Funeral companies will sell you caskets, cemetery plots,
headstones, and markers to support a burial. Families who choose
cremation have few options on how they commemorate a life 
Kinwood makes short films to celebrate lives. The client family provides
photos, cell phone video and 4-8 names for a Kinwood interview. Then
our film editors take the interview footage, photos, home movies, cell
phone video and produce a beautiful 20–30-minute commemorative film
that will remain in the family forever.
These films are often played at a Celebration of Life. Some families that
are separated by great distances choose to make a Kinwood film instead
of a funeral.
Kinwood understands that families and friends want to “do something” to
honor a life. This is it. Part family history, part commemoration, a Kinwood
Commemorative Film becomes an instant heirloom and a centerpiece for
any Celebration of Life.

Yes. Today, the cremation rate in the USA is 60% - In the 1960ʼs, it was
5%. Since then, funeral companies have wanted to sell you elaborate
funerals – big ones with limousines, fancy caskets, and majestic
ceremonies at a church or funeral home.
Attitudes and values around death-care and end-of-life issues are rapidly
changing. Many families now walk into a funeral home, ask for a
cremation, and tell the funeral director, “We are just going to take the urn
and go and do our own thing” – typically at a club, park, restaurant, or a
special location like a boxing gym. Kinwood films are for families who are
questioning the traditional funeral business.
The Funeral: The product that never
improves
Many funeral homes say they are long-time local businesses, but in fact,
25% of them are now owned and controlled by private equity and
conglomerates. Private equity has a track-record of investing in industries
to increase profits at the expense of customer service levels. Families are
leaving the category because they believe that big-funeral companies care
most about upselling services that people donʼt want or need.
So, why havenʼt funeral companies come up with new and better
products? Well, the industry is frozen as private equity, and
conglomerates are preoccupied with buying independent funeral homes.
Many independent funeral homes are preoccupied with being acquired.
No-one is trying to make this better. So unless you want a big traditional
funeral with all the trimmings, families have few meaningful options.
You can help Kinwood Films lead the change for more meaningful
commemorations and change the entire funeral industry for the better.
Preserving the stories of a life
Whether you watch a Kinwood Commemorative Film on a big screen or re-
watch it again on your phone, the stories, sentiment, and feelings of deep
connection can be preserved. Many of our films are re-watched dozens of
times – and often become the go-to option when you need to go back in
time and relive these memories.

After a few weeks of looking at the funeral industry, my enthusiasm for the
subject grew. I thought there was an opportunity for a new funeral
company that was about celebrating lives with modern products and
services. After 3-months of kicking it around, I came to my senses and
deemed it foolish to quit my marketing career and start a company. I
shelved the idea.
About 12 weeks later, one of my closest friends, Jody, 44, was diagnosed
with pancreatic cancer and died in just 6 weeks. Total shock. Family and
friends devastated. For his celebration of life, my friend Jesse set up a
camera at the back of the pub and he did short interviews with friends,
asking about their experiences and reflections with Jody. Everyone wanted
to share their memories and Jesse ran the camera for 3 hours.
We then got photo albums from his mom, and Jodyʼs friends sent cell-
phone pics and videos. Gerrit cut the whole thing and that was our pilot
film.
We got our first job through an obituary cold call, and eventually, several
more trickled in. We worked with a couple of funeral homes who loved our
work, and they made some referrals.
Then, by accident, our MAJOR breakthrough happened. Instead of
shooting interviews in person with nice cameras and lenses, a client
insisted we use video conferencing. With some new tricks ( thanks Gerrit!)
we did 10 remote interviews and the film was still amazing! We have
completed several more cloud films – (thanks Mel!).
These films are beloved by families – and become an important heirloom.
Like the coolest obituary around.
That should get you up to speed.
The Kinwood Team
Our original business idea was to apply the skills of professional creatives
to the very un-creative funeral industry. Today, Kinwood is a small team of
creative and advertising professionals - who mostly live in different cities.
This is us.

Specifically, your funds will be used to pay for a software engineer to build

By backing this campaign, Kinwood will deliver the ability for anyone,

Risks and challenges
Kinwoodʼs team has years of professional experience in production and
creative delivery – and we know how to make these amazing films. We are
planning to hire a skilled software engineer – or two - and hopefully bring
them in-house to Kinwood.
As with any tech-build project, we recognize the potential for challenges
during the construction of this digital platform. Our commitment is to have
outside advisors review our technical plans, to minimize any unforeseen
risks. This is Kinwoodʼs first Kickstarter campaign and we are committed
to developing the best possible experience for bereaved families.
Our pledge is to keep our backer community updated on our progress and
be transparent if challenges develop.
To be honest – we are not sure what kind of reaction this will get…. Our
biggest risk might be meeting the demand this new service will generate.
