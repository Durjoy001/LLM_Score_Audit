The Story of Conneco
Conneco is a platform designed to integrate educational resources for
children aged 4-16 from various ethnic backgrounds, fostering a space
where they can learn and grow together. The inspiration for Conneco
came from its founder, a single mother, who faced significant challenges in
finding suitable courses for her 6.5-year-old child. She found the process
time-consuming and costly and fraught with the high costs of trial and
error.
Determined to make a difference, this mother collaborated with several
MBA classmates and education professors to develop Conneco. The
platform aggregates high-quality educational resources, helping parents
and children easily find appropriate courses, thus significantly reducing the
time and money spent on searching and selecting them.
Each child can save $2000-$3000 annually in learning expenses through
Conneco. These savings can be reinvested in the child's education or
used for living expenses, easing the financial burden on families.
Conneco not only provides an efficient and convenient platform for
educational resources but also brings hope and support to parents and
children, allowing them to focus more on learning and growth. Through
this platform, children from diverse backgrounds can learn together,
explore the vast ocean of knowledge, and create a brighter future.
