How We Protect Your Data
Capture – myinsightiq records and transcribes your speech in real time,
processing the data securely through AI-powered speech-to-text
technology. Audio data is not stored and is deleted within seconds after
transcription.
Local Storage – Transcripts are encrypted and stored securely on your
device, preventing unauthorized access.
Transmission & Sharing – myinsightiq only transmits transcripts when you
choose to share them. You can collaborate across up to 10 platforms,
including WhatsApp, LinkedIn, Zoom, Discord, Google, Slack, and more. If
you opt to use third-party AI services, transcripts will be sent only upon
your request. In the future, we will support multiple AI providers, giving you
the flexibility to choose AI services that align with your privacy
preferences.
