About
Mobile app to track and forecast people's CO2 emissions caused by their
housing, consumption habits, mode of transportation (bicycle, car, plane,
etc.) and trip distances (tracked automatically by changes in the
smartphone's gps coordinates, the app questions the user how he/she
traveled once immobile after each trip). Then, the user has the option to
offset some or all (0% to 100%) of those CO2 emissions with integrated
payments to carbon offset providers like bullfrogpower.com or
carbonfund.org, or to user's green mutual funds.
The web platform will have an open API so that third-parties (government,
stores or companies) can integrate NoCO2 to: 1) reward users
(citizens/clients or employees) on their low CO2 emissions by matching the
users' contributions to offset providers or to green mutual funds, 2) upload
data on all kinds of vehicles', houses' or products' CO2 emissions throughout
their whole life cycle (production to disposal).
The goal here is to get people to change their habits (purchases, activities,
etc.) to KEEP OUR PLANET AS OUR HOME FOREVER!
