Imagine the joy on a child's face when they open their own personalized

Storyay is dedicated to nurturing children's creativity and love for
literature through personalized, mail-based letters. At a time when
children's enjoyment of reading is at an all-time low, we're on a mission
to make reading exciting, immersive, and accessible for kids aged 7-12.
Our personalized letters, created by local writers and illustrators—not AI
—connect with children in a meaningful, screen-free way.
Join us to give a child the gift of reading, whether they're in your
family, a friend or a child in need. Be the hero who sparks a lifelong
love of stories!
Why Storyay Matters
Reading for pleasure in childhood is more than just a pastime—it has
been shown to improve cognitive performance, mental well-being, and
school success. But here's the reality: fewer children than ever enjoy
reading today, especially boys and older kids. Research has shown that
children need shorter-form stories with stories and characters to which
they can relate and develop a love of reading. Storyay makes reading
something to look forward to, a unique adventure that arrives just for
them in the mail.
As CEO of Schoolio and a parent myself, I know how important it is to
develop a love of literacy for our kids. Kids love getting mail,
especially when it's a personalized message just for them, complete
with illustrations and activities. Storyay makes reading an adventure
with the child in the center.

What Backers Receive: A Magical,
Ongoing Story Experience
As a Storyay backer, you'll receive a beautifully illustrated invitation to
gift. Here's how the Storyay experience unfolds:
Receive the Storyay Box
For select perks that include the full Storyay, the child will receive an
illustrated Storyay box in the mail containing an introduction to each
story. The child will use these introduction letters to choose which story
to receive for their subscription. The box is designed with fun activities
and branded items to enjoy. This is where the excitement truly begins.
Choose the Story
The child you've chosen will explore Storyay's three fully written and
illustrated launch stories and receive a gift invitation to go online (with
adult supervision). They'll pick the story they connect with the most,
choose a boy or a girl protagonist, and provide personal details to make
the Storyay uniquely theirs.
