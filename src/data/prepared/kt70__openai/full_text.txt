Risks and challenges
Every ambitious product carries real risks. We believe you deserve to know
ours.
1. Screen-awareness is technically hard.
Teaching an AI to understand any screen — every app, every layout, every
edge case — is the core challenge. Our early builds handle the common
80% beautifully. Closing the last 20% (niche software, unusual UIs, low-
resolution screens) will take continued training, user feedback, and time.
2. Accuracy is everything — and we're not perfect yet.
HyberX will sometimes misread a screen or give guidance that isn't quite
right. We're transparent about this. Every response is clearly marked as AI-
generated, and we're building confidence indicators so you always know
when to double-check. Trust is earned, and we're earning it.
3. Earning trust around privacy takes more than a promise.
Asking people to let an AI see their screen is a big ask. Even though
HyberX only activates on demand and never records in the background,
we know skepticism is healthy. We're pursuing independent security audits
and publishing our data-handling practices openly.
4. Scaling real-time AI is expensive.
Context-aware, real-time guidance costs significantly more to run than a
standard chatbot. As we grow, infrastructure costs grow with us. Your
backing directly helps us maintain response speed and quality without
compromising the experience.
