I do. As a Canadian, I always wish Kickstarter have the option to list the
project in different currency, you may feel the same if you are outside of
USA.
It is not convenient for backers to convert your currency to their own
currencies.
Last month during my project, I found one project was very clever, they put
approximate amount in other currencies directly in the rewards. I used the
same technique in my project:
https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/786298545/tapusb-one-tap-to-
switch-on-off-the-power-from-a-u
It is a good idea, but time consuming to do it for 12 rewards. Since I know
programming, why not create a software to do it!
A software can generate approximate
amount in other currencies for
your rewards. All you need to do is
copy&paste.
Here is the link: http://reward-currency-generator.appspot.com/
