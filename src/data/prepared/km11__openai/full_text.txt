I'm Canadian, so this game is from the Frozen Beards.
I have 13 years of experience making software, and I always had a
passion for video games. I have worked on small video game projects
over the years, but this time I want to make one that I can grow.
This is a project in which I will add features based on the players and
the community.
The plan
The vision is to focus on racing, beating your own high score, beating
others high scores, and making new race tracks.
The game should take around a year to build. After that, it will be
released in early access. By then, most of the gameplay and
customization options will have been implemented. The game will also
contain enough content to be enjoyable. During the early access, more
content will be added, and the comments of the community will be
listened to in order to find what they like and what needs to be
improved.

Risks and challenges
The biggest risk would be that the game is delayed. It could happen if I
fall sick, but that is unlikely.
It is my first project that I release on Steam. I will have to get used to its
procedure to be able to deliver it to you. Many have done it before, so it
should be a nice challenge.
