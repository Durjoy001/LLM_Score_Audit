Story
withEase (wE) is a comprehensive Platform as a Service (PaaS) providing a
protected identity layer in any Internet collaboration. wE uses Artificial
Intelligence (AI) and simple conversational interface to control, protect
and monetize personal and proprietary information.
As a person, you can get paid for:
exposure to ads based on your own preferences and careabouts
your AI's responses to market research queries
your AI's participation in polls based on your own profile
intellectual property in new skills defined by you
As an enterprise, you can augment the dreaded IT lifecycle and
implement new skills in a manner of minutes.

Service Catalog
Over the years of development, wE has accumulated thousands of internal
services. Anywhere from a trivial "convert this text to lower case" to
resource-demanding "export the entire content of wEvatar and ship it to
owner".
wE also has a nomenclature, a framework which AI uses in process of app
generation from policy text, or to be more specific, from meta skill. Like,
properties of service input parameters (a.i. typed, mandatory vs. optional,
etc.) and operational parameters (a.i. public vs. propitiatory, resource
allocations, billing parameters, etc.) However, this framework was finalized
relatively recently creating a legacy of thousands of already available
services.
We need your help to allocate proper resources to (a) retrofit legacy
services and (b) teach wE AI as to their intent. Again, we are targeting a
period of three months.

wE IS NOT A BUSINESS, YET
To this point, all our effort were focused on development. Occasional early
adopting customers contributed financially and, more importantly with
advice and guidance. Nevertheless, wE does not have any discernible
business infrastructure like sales, marketing, customer support, etc. Just
product development. With the proceeds of this project, we intend to keep
it the same. Future business infrastructure will be financed through
different means.
SCALE
We cannot predict the level of backers' interest in our project. While we do
believe current platform can handle very large number of simultaneous
users, we will be tracking this issue very closely.
SPAM
wE uses enhanced email for notifications. While each email is branded for
a single communication with a single user and with personalized encoded
keys, potential for SPAM is always a concern. wE's active security features
are designed to detect SPAM and, as an initial measure, notify potential
offenders accordingly.
WITH CONSUMER IN MIND
Arguably, wE can be of benefit to both consumer and enterprise.
Consumer gets personal AI, which they don't currently have. Enterprise
augments IT, which they dread.
Our strong preference is that the future of wE gets to be driven primarily
by consumers. Therefore, your support for this project would mean much
more than just the monetary value of your backing.
