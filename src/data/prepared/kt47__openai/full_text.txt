Hello all and thank you for taking a moment to check out my project. My
name is Jonathan Stevanka, I am 24 years old from Ontario,Canada with
May 26 2023 - Jun 25 2023
a passion for taking risks and doing things no one else really tries to do. (30 days)
This lead me to the development of Rewind, what started off as a pet
project making a instagram clone to "replace" the social media giant

quickly turned into much more. I quickly thought why not take
everyones favourite features from every social media app and plug
them all in one. This paired with the distrust everybody has today with
big tech owned platforms it seemed like a great time to start building my
own.
Rewind early wireframe, V1.0 design 2020
When I started building this app it was while I was still going to school
for "Mobile Applications development" so I grew up very much on social
media and because I have a tech background I paid close attention to
what the big dogs are doing.
I quickly realized there is a large percentage of users who are unhappy
with instagram but wont leave because there is no competitor that is
quite like it. This lead me to start an instagram(ironic) account
rewind.app to see how many other users would be interested in this.

May 26th 2023 - Testflight alpha testing
Now with an app of this nature your immediate thought was probably,
how in the world is this guy going to fund this? So far the app is free to
use for everyone, however in order to reduce the upkeep we will be
putting out donator Tier levels from 1-3 each having similar but different
perks. This will allow users who want to help out and support the
chance but also giving them something back, I had to get very creative
compared to modern social media tactics but I think mine in the end will
prevail if given time.
Another plan is also business submitted advertisement applications, IE
paying to have a video of your business promoted on the app in a
certain area or for a certain demographic.
The final and last option is investors, with an app of this scale It is
inevitable. I am open and looking please contact me and we can speak.
So with all that in mind I set out over the course of 3 and something
years and built Rewind from a "pet project" into the start of a social
media app. There has been a lot done inside the app up until this point,
but in the next two-three months its going to feel like I am going to have
to run around the world. I am under quite the time crunch to get it
released but I love a challenge!
Thanks for reading!
