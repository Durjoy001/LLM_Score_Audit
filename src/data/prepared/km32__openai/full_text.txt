Ever since the advent of computer-based music, composers spend
precious minutes of their day just trying to get the right sound to play back.
Montreal Music Labs is creating a singular solution for all music 
composers and producers with their computers to control and edit virtual
instrument articulations and key switches.
It works with all major third party libraries and players, including Kontakt,
VSL, Play, and more.
Fresh off of MML's first major release VM1 (an iPad controller application
for VSL's Vienna Ensemble Pro 7) we are now concentrating on simplifying
yet another crucial part of the composer workflow.
Creating Music Should be Fun!
Montreal Music Labs creates technical products for creative people. We
know from experience that technology can be a roadblock when you want
to just sit down and write music. We're here to simplify how people make
music on computers.

Runs on an iPad*
All articulations, easy to understand.*
MML's Articulate is an integrated AU/VST/AAX plugin and touchscreen app
solution that gives Music Composers quick control with minimal user
configuration.
Remembering where each instrumentʼs articulations are on the keyboard is
a daunting & tedious task. With Articulate, all of your articulations appear
on the iPad as simple buttons. Just select the desired articulation and it
will be stored as automation - not MIDI - in your DAW. This allows for
much easier editing and for standard automation functions such as touch,

Timeline
The preliminary work has been completed and we are currently refining
the product code to make this work smoothly and easily for all our users.
We will be releasing a beta version of the application to our testers in early
2020 and plan to officially release to all users in March 2020. Introductory
