Risks and challenges
Business Software is an amazing, powerful option to make your business
more efficient and capable and to offer your customers power and novel
ways to engage with you which bring them enormous value.
However, a business software project is an expensive and risky undertaking
and a badly failed business software project can end up producing nothing
of value at great expense.
Here are the three ways, we'll use, to reduce risk on our software
development project and make sure to end up with a viable, positive
outcome.
1. Taking Small Bites: Minimum Viable Product
Getting real-world feedback early means we have an opportunity to measure
real response to our software.
2. Identifing and Addressing Technical Risks Early.
By verifying early that technical challenges are solvable, we greatly reduce
the risk of large failures late in the project when weʼve made a large
investment.
