Super Cursor is a challenging precision platformer where you control your
very own mouse cursor, who is brought to life and given his own
personality! Move through the world as you dodge obstacles and enemies
who are stopping you from reaching your beloved princess.
Be careful though, as anything that you touch will kill you instantly and you
will have to start all over again!
Features:
Dodge obstacles, enemies, and projectiles thrown your way.
Original characters, soundtracks and world.
Online Co-op because two heads are better than one right?
Bring personality to your cursor.
Funny, satirical, and rich dialogue.
Become a time traveler?

Super Cursor has been developed mostly by myself, Zenfuso. The Story,
design, gameplay, characters, and dialogue were created with my utmost
effort. It has been my dream for years to create a game that others can
enjoy and play together. This is my first official project and I thank every
one of you for supporting this journey!
Risks and challenges
Since this game is being developed by only myself, there are quite a lot to
learn throughout the development of this game. There are very real risks of
delays and setbacks and I want any backer to know of this risk. I will do my
best to get the game and rewards out on time to keep everyone as
satisfied as possible. The game is currently planned to be released in early
2025.
