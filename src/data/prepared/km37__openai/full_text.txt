DESCRIPTION
We admire the effort put into QuarkXPress 2016, for its stability,
performance, and above all its new capability to convert custom files into
Quark Native Objects. We took a glimpse at the QuarkXPress software
stack and found it to be very promising, and jumped on the bandwagon to
develop XTensions.
NEW CAPABILITIES
Imagine the ability to create an entire QuarkXPress project from just a PDF
File! The PDF Importer leverages the QuarkXPress 2016 “Convert To Native
Objects” feature to get the job done for you.
