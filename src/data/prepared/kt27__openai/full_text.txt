Risks and challenges
Building a category-defining product like Kora—a wall-mounted AI
assistant designed specifically for busy families—comes with real
challenges.

sourcing components, quality control, and logistics—especially as global
supply chains remain unpredictable.

manufacturing partner and built working prototypes. We're planning small-
batch production first to identify and resolve issues early.
