Tipcast is for sharing a story that develops over time
Many stories unfold over days, weeks, months and even years. With
Tipcast, you can watch other stories unfold over time, or create your own
story. Each update to a story is a chapter 15 seconds long, making it super
easy and fast to publish updates on the go directly from your smartphone.
Upload your photos
Zoom and pan through to focus on details
Add simple text overlays
Add a soundtrack from our royalty-free music library
Add voice narration by recording with your smartphone microphone
Simple tools to create or update a Tipcast

What's Our Story?
My name is Josh Barkin and I've been working with a team building mobile
apps for years. We built reward apps for AT&T, Sprint, and Virgin Mobile
USA/Canada. More recently we focused on iPhone apps. Along the way,
I've stopped exercising, drank several cans of coke and a lot of coffee. I've
got into some pretty nasty eating habits. I'm hoping to change my routine
and document my story. I think I'll be more motivated to reach my goals if
I publish my story as it unfolds. My team is going to give everyone the
tools to share a developing story. Whether it's a personal project, a
business project, or a local news story you want to share, or follow.
Milestones to Date
We built a prototype but have much work to do. We'd like to launch
Tipcast in the iTunes App Store by February 2014. We're truly grateful and
thankful to all of you who can help make Tipcast more than just a great
way to tell a story, but a platform to share progress, one chapter at a time,
and lead to happy endings. Thank you from team Stagename!
Risks and challenges
Over the past year, we worked hard to understand our technical limitations
through a number of prototypes and iterations. We're confident that both
your feedback and actual usage data will help guide our decisions, and so
we can continue to deliver great results. We're just as determined to build
on a product we can use for our own projects as we are for everyone else!
