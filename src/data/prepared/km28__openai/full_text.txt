Firstly, there is nothing like this that exists online. My website prototype, is
at http://www.petspectation.com and I programmed a new concept for Pet
Food delivery nationally.
The concept is simple: Pay $39 a month and as a member, you request
and get delivered to you, on your schedule, all the dog or cat food you
need.
And, as a paid member, you'll also be able to send deliveries of dog or cat
food to your friends anywhere in the U.S. or Canada as well for free.
Petspectation.com (still in a prototype beta mode) is a website I own and
am in the process of programming which is a Pet Food aggregator website
powered by software and logic that connects premium pet food fulfillment
centers (i.e. traditionally called "suppliers"!) with overstock or inventory
they wish to liquidate or sell at a very reduced rate to a vast (hopefully)
bank of paid consumer members who, for a monthly fee, starting at $39
per month, paid monthly members can request unlimited deliveries of
available pet food (dog or cat food) or no additional fee of pet food
immediately available to ship for one monthly fee. 1 bag at a time, and
when they receive their shipment, two business days later, schedule
another delivery. There is no additional fees, just the $39 per month to join
the dog food plan, or cat food plan, or $59 a month for both the dog and
cat food plan. Prices are in U.S. Dollars and are subject to change and our
fulfillment partners (i.e. suppliers) will be located in the USA and Canada
are shipping to the USA and Canada for shipments destined to be
delivered to any USA or Canadian address.
Consumers who wish to be members subscribe to a plan, and only pay
$39 a month and as a member, they can request as many deliveries as
they'd like, respecting the terms and conditions, one bag at time, shipped
