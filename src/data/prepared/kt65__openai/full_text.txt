In February 2024, this photo was taken of me with my mom and dad
in front of Niagara Falls. They obviously look like they are shivering
from the bitter cold, (though my smile tells a different story!). What
the photo doesnʼt show is the 15-minute walk we had to take after
circling endlessly for parking.
That frustration, discomfort, and wasted time stuck with me. Itʼs
exactly moments like these that inspired the creation of this app—
because finding parking shouldnʼt ruin the experience of the
destination.
So, I did what any frustrated builder would do: I sketched, designed, and
built a (very) basic version of an app that could solve this. I called it
Park Project.

Risks and challenges
While weʼve already built core parts of the app and are handling primary
development in-house, there are still a few challenges weʼre preparing
for:
🚧 Platform Integration Complexity
Integrating with CarPlay, Android Auto, and Wear OS requires approval
and strict UI compliance from Apple and Google. Weʼre working with
experienced developers to streamline this and follow all best practices.
📍 Real-time Parking Data Accuracy
Since ParkProject relies on community-driven and updated data,
accuracy depends on user participation. To solve this, weʼre building
strong incentives through our Park Coins system and real time checks.
🧪 Beta Feedback & Testing
Launching across multiple devices and car platforms introduces
variations in behavior. Weʼll roll out in waves and closely monitor user
feedback to fix any bugs quickly.
⚖ Location-Based Compliance
Handling location data responsibly is key. Weʼve planned for basic legal
reviews and will follow all privacy regulations (including Canadian & U.S.
guidelines).
