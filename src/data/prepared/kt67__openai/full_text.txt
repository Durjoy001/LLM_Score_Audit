Our Story
We are two seasoned Project Managers that have been around the
corporate world for over three decades (you can guess...we are Baby
Boomers !!!!).
At the beginning of our careers we suffered the old style of doing things:
no clear targets, every day changing priorities, coordination problems,
organization silos, scarce team collaboration, ....
As a result we worked too many hours, had little time for family, and
friends --> an unbalanced life, and yes, we regret it. A that time we had no
options.
So, each of us in parallel, searched for any tool to improve our quality of
life; the management of the famous work-personal divide.
The cloud era offered us many applications. New apps appeared each year
and then each month. So each year/month we renewed our quest for the
“magical tool”.
We tried them all: Gantt based PM apps, ToDo lists,
Mindmaps, Group Collaboration Task Management Systems, KanBan
boards and, because it is popular, Slack too. Yes, surely they are better
compared to e-mail, but not what a "PM geek" needs.
4 years ago we meet by chance and shared our experiences. We joined
forces and started to research for a real solution. We wanted to leave our
mark in this busy world giving the new generations a new way of doing
meaningful things. It is something we really care of, our challenge.
As a result of our research we were "human work activity" experts, but

7/13/26, 3:52 PM Zensmith - the "Life Operating System" by Zensmith — Kickstarter
To fulfill the functional principles, Zensmith is organized in 3 layers:
1st layer: the Project Space is a P2P network. The PM creates a project in
his "workspace" and invites the team members and followers.
2nd layer: the Task's Space supports tasks' structuring, assignments and
dependencies management. Allows to match the workflow tho the activity
life cycle.
3rd layer: Visual Management layer that allows the user to have a macro
and micro vision of the activities he/she is involved in. Enables the user to
decide what action to do next according to the actual context.
