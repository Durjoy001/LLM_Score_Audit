Love music? Colorkeys was conceived more than ten years ago by a days)
teacher who wanted a way to pass on his love of piano and music to his
students. Traditional music classes for an hour a week just didnʼt reach
most kids and usually ended with more frustration than enjoyment. He

needed a way to quickly teach full classes of students to learn how to play
piano and read music in a fun, engaging, and academically sound way.
Colorkeys was invented to solve this problem.
Colorkeys is based on a patented color-coded note system where music
and notes are represented in colors that correspond to colored stickers
that are used on music keys. This helps kids quickly make the connection
between the notes on the page and the keys on the keyboard. Using this
simple, easily understood concept, kids can begin to learn in a few hours
what usually takes months of lessons to achieve –even for kids as young
as five.
We spent many years researching the most effective piano methodology
which we developed into a software program designed to aid a teacher in a
classroom setting. The program lets kids learn at their own pace and
provides instant feedback and rewards to promote learning. Over the past
five years, the program has grown quickly and is now a proven teaching
tool that is being used by dozens of schools and thousands of kids
throughout the United States and Canada.
Why We Need You:
We are a small company that has had success with our school based
program. There are thousands of students using the program, but its
exposure is limited to the schools offering Colorkeys in their curriculum.
There are millions of kids who will never even be exposed to playing music
because of budget cutbacks at their schools and money concerns at
home. As educators and as music lovers we find this unacceptable!

Overview of How the Game Will Work:
Designed with the dual purpose of both fun and sound academic
principles, the video game will appeal to kids and encourage them to move
forward and keep growing in their skills. They will be required to finish
tasks and stages that kids are used to seeing in games before moving on
to more material. A point system that will be based on finishing activities
and accurate playing will encourage their natural competitive behavior to
achieve better and better results. Kids will not even realize that they are
doing what all music teachers want from their students – namely, practice!
They will be mastering songs and understanding theoretical material all
through playing the game.
The levels of the software will be sequential; students canʼt move ahead
until they have mastered a concept. Teaching new concepts is done
through short, instructional videos that are reinforced through the
gameplay. Concept understanding is practiced with drag-and-drop
activities and tested with periodic, short quizzes.
Help Us Help Our Schools
We are serious about our commitment to enhance music education and
make it accessible to everyone. A big part of what we do is to help
educators in our schools teach music to kids. That is why after this game is
developed (with your help!), we will use the game to help give back to
school music education. For every 100 games that are sold, we will donate
a Colorkeys school program package to a school in need (feel free to email
us with worthy suggestions). This will include everything a school needs to
make a music program a reality, including the Colorkeys educational
software, up to 30 digital keyboards, Colorkeys educational materials, and
teacher training. In order to make that happen we need your support.
Please help us develop this game!
