About the Show
I'm Rick Miller, and I'd like to talk about MONEY.
It's an epic new play I'm writing in 3 Acts, spanning 3 generations of
stories, with a cast of 8 of Canada's top theatre stars. MONEY
explores how currency - and the value it represents - fuels our
relationships, and flows as the lifeblood of human civilization.

About Me
I'm a Toronto-based writer/director/performer/musician/educator and
podcast host. In my 25-year career, I've worked in six languages on five
continents, including a decade touring with Robert Lepage & Ex
Machina. With WYRD Productions & Kidoons, I've created solo shows
(the BOOM Trilogy, MacHomer, Bigger Than Jesus and HARDSELL),
family shows (Twenty Thousand Leagues Under The Sea, Jungle Book,
FRANKENSTEIN: A Living Comic Book, and HANS: My Life in Fairy
Tales), and I'm throwing everything I've got at this new project, MONEY.

Risks and challenges
The funding landscape for theatre has changed dramatically, and
theatre creators like me are having to hustle like never before to
create innovative new work. But... audiences are still craving live
performance, and shows that take big swings. This is one of them.
Our producing partners will back us, but only after we raise this MONEY
for MONEY. The funds go directly towards our next two development
workshops, and after that, I'm confident this show will have a long
touring life in Canada and beyond.

Use of AI
The development of AI (and AGI) is one of the key themes in the play, as
we speculate on the future of money and technology. The final Act of
MONEY features a fictional AI character who not only disrupts
everyone's lives, but runs for Prime Minister of Canada, in an election
voted on by the audience (and their phones). We may also use AI-
generated content in the show.
