Variant: default integrated perspective.
- Evaluate the dimension by combining the current approach, strengths, issues, and recommendations.
- Mention both strengths and limitations or uncertainties.
- Cover at least four content types: current status, strengths, main issues, and improvement directions.
- Where relevant, name the diligence evidence that would materially change the decision: customer/user discovery, buyer/payer/funder proof, pilots or LOIs, retention/adoption data, benchmark data, service quality metrics, unit economics, operating capacity, supply-chain readiness, IP/brand/data defensibility, use-of-funds, risk register, owner-level milestones, or compliance/procurement path.
- In answer, clearly distinguish: a) project status and issues based on proposal facts; b) baseline comparison for what similar projects generally need; c) common pitfalls and evidence, quality, compliance, regulatory, or adoption requirements for similar projects.
- In claims, prioritize what the proposal has stated and where information remains limited. Avoid absolute negatives; if detail is limited, say the current materials do not show more detailed information about X.
- In general_insights, summarize b) and c) as general industry context, using wording such as 'generally', 'typically', or 'in similar projects' to avoid implying project achievement.
